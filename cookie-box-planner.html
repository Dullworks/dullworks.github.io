Plan every cookie box from your own recipes

Paste your own recipes, set how many boxes you're giving and what goes in each, and the planner does the rest. It works out how many whole batches to make, builds a dated plan for mixing, freezing, thawing and baking that counts back from your giving day, and gives you one combined shopping list in store pack sizes.

What it does

  • Paste ingredient lines like "2 1/4 cups flour" or "1 cup (2 sticks) butter". You can fix anything in an editable table.
  • Set one or more box sizes, the number of boxes, and how many of each cookie go in each box. Add a buffer for breakage and taste-testing.
  • Batches are rounded up to whole batches, and spare cookies are shown.
  • A day-by-day plan counts back from giving day, using the keep and freeze times you enter and a limit on batches per day.
  • One shopping list merges all recipes, grouped by aisle, with US pack sizes such as pounds of butter, dozens of eggs and 12 oz bags of chips.
  • Prints "what's inside" cards for each box, plus the schedule and the shopping list.
  • Saves in your browser automatically, with no account. Back up and restore with one file. Includes a fictional example plan.

What you get

  • cookie-box-planner.html, one file that works offline
  • A 3-page PDF quick-start guide

Good to know

  • Tested in Chrome. It uses standard browser features, so it should work in other current browsers.
  • Pack sizes are typical US sizes. Ingredients it doesn't know are listed as you wrote them.
  • You enter your own keep and freeze times. The planner gives no food-safety advice.
  • The schedule plans oven batches per day, not oven temperatures or decorating time.
