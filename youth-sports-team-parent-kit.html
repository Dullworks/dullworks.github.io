Keep the team list and the rotation without asking every family to join an app

A parent or coach who just got asked to run snacks, sideline duties and the roster can put the season in one offline file. Add the families and the schedule, then build a fair rotation. Print the binder pages, or send a plain text list. Households take one turn, even when two siblings are on the team.

What it does

  • Roster and contacts by family and player, so two siblings share one household turn, not two.
  • Game, meet, competition and practice schedule, plus a weekly series tool for a regular slot.
  • A fair snack and duty rotator that spreads turns evenly across families. It avoids giving the same family the same duty in two weeks in a row when another family is free, and it tries not to give one family two duties on the same day.
  • Skips for a family who is away, and locked swaps when two families trade turns. Rebuilds keep those locks.
  • Duty types you can edit: snacks, sideline or bench duty, a score table, a team bag, or your own.
  • Works for basketball, hockey, indoor soccer, cheer, swimming, or any other youth sport you name. No league or club names.
  • Printable binder cover, roster, schedule, filled rotation, blank sign-up sheets and a game-day checklist, on US Letter or A4.
  • A plain text rotation list you can download and send to families.
  • Saves in your browser automatically, with no account. Back up and restore with one file. Includes a fictional 12-family winter basketball example, and a blank start.

What you get

  • team-parent-kit.html, one file that works offline
  • A ready-made printable set in US Letter and A4, filled in from the example team: cover, roster, schedule, rotation, sign-up sheets and a game-day checklist
  • A 1-page PDF quick-start guide

Good to know

  • Tested in Google Chrome. Other browsers were not tested.
  • Print at Actual size or 100%.
  • Planning and printing only. It is not medical, safety or coaching advice, and it is not a league or club system. Do not store medical notes here.
  • Names and phone numbers stay in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them, so keep a backup file.
