Thank everyone who is not family, from one list

In December there can be ten or more people to thank besides family: class teachers, aides, the bus driver, coaches, daycare staff, the sitter, the mail carrier. Type their names once. The kit gives each person a give-by date, shows who is overdue, and prints matching gift card holders and tags with their name on them.

What it does

  • 18 helper roles ready to tick, from class teacher and bus driver to dog walker and hair stylist, plus rows for anyone else.
  • For each person: name, role, which kids it is from, gift type, your own planned amount, give-by date, and ticks for bought, given and thank-you note sent.
  • Enter the last school day and the last daycare, activity or home visit before Christmas. School roles take the first date, the others take the second, and any row can have its own date.
  • A give-by list sorted by date, with overdue rows in red and an overdue-only view.
  • A summary by kid and by role group, plus who is not bought or not given yet.
  • Print studio with 8 original designs. Each holder says "To" with the name, a thank-you line for that role (18 lines written in-house, and you can change any of them) and "From" with your kids' names. Holders print 2 per page, matching tags print 8 per page, and a one-page checklist prints too.
  • Holders fold and glue into a sleeve that fits a standard 3.375 x 2.125 in gift card.
  • A class gift page for room parents: families, invited, pledged, received, method, date and thanked, with amounts blurred on screen. The printable report-back note shows the total, what was bought and what is left over, never what one family gave.
  • Short factual notes: the USPS gift rule on mail carrier rows, and a reminder to check your school or district gift policy on school rows. No suggested amounts.
  • Saves in your browser with no account. Back up and restore with one file. Includes a fictional example.

What you get

  • thank-you-kit.html, one file that works offline
  • Printable PDFs for US Letter and A4: 8 blank holder designs, 18 role-line holders, 24 tags, a paper checklist and a class gift sheet. The To and From lines are fillable form fields, or you can write on them.
  • A 1-page quick-start guide

Good to know

  • Tested in Google Chrome. Other browsers were not tested.
  • Print at Actual size or 100%. Card stock works best. You cut, fold and glue the holders yourself.
  • Made for helpers outside the family. It is not a family gift list.
  • Recordkeeping and printing only. No amounts, tax or financial advice.
  • Your list saves in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes it, so keep a backup file.
