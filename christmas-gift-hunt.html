A Christmas morning present hunt for every kid at once

Each kid gets their own colour, their own trail and clues they can read. Tick the hiding spots you really have, and the builder gives every kid a separate trail, so nobody races a sibling to the same spot. Then print the clue cards, a parent hide sheet and an answer key.

What it does

  • Add 1 to 4 kids. Give each one a colour (red, green, gold or blue), an icon (star, tree, bell or snowflake) and 4 to 10 clues.
  • Pick a reading level for each kid: picture clues for non-readers, rhymes for early readers, riddles for about 8 and up, or a secret letter-shift code with a decoder strip for about 11 and up.
  • Tick spots from 65 built-in hiding places in 8 rooms, and add up to 10 of your own with your own clue text.
  • Builds the trails so no two kids share a hiding spot. Trails move from room to room, and the last clue leads to a gift spot for each kid or to one shared big gift spot (the only spot trails can share). Press Reshuffle for a new mix.
  • Every card shows the name, colour, icon and "3 of 7", so cards from different trails are easy to tell apart, even on a black and white printer.
  • Prints clue cards 8 per page on US Letter or A4, start envelope cards, finish certificates and blank cards.
  • The parent hide sheet lists each trail in reverse order, with one walk-through list by room and an answer key for the morning.
  • Saves in your browser automatically, with no account. Back up and restore with one file. Includes a fictional 3-kid example.

What you get

  • gift-hunt-builder.html, one file that works offline
  • A ready-made printable set in US Letter and A4 (19 pages each): 3 rhyme trails and 1 picture trail of 8 cards, blank cards in 4 colours, the hide sheet, the answer key and certificates
  • A 1-page PDF quick-start guide

Good to know

  • Tested in Google Chrome. It uses standard browser printing, so it should work in other current browsers.
  • All clue text and pictures are original, with no characters or brand names.
  • You choose the spots, so check that each one is safe and easy to reach for your kids. The builder keeps picture-level kids out of the garage and outside spots when it can.
  • The ready-made set uses common spots. Use the blank cards for any spot you do not have.
