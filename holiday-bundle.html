Gifts and cookie boxes, planned in one place

Two planners for the busy part of December. The gift tracker keeps one shared gift list for two adults and shows each child's gift count and spending side by side. The cookie box planner takes your own recipes and works out the batches, a dated bake-and-freeze plan and one shopping list.

What's inside

Two-Parent Christmas Gift Tracker

  • Gifts tab for up to 300 gifts, with who's buying (adult A or B), budget, actual cost, store, status and hiding spot.
  • Kid balance panel for up to 8 kids: gift count and spend side by side, the gap to the top child, and a "behind" flag past a threshold you set.
  • Stockings tab, and an orders and returns log for up to 200 orders with "late for gift date" and return-window flags.
  • A one-page printable summary of budget against spend.
  • Blank and fictional-example .xlsx workbooks with no macros, plus a 3-page setup guide (PDF).

Cookie Box Planner

  • Paste your own recipes and fix anything in an editable ingredient table.
  • Set box sizes, box counts and cookies per box. Batches round up to whole batches, and spare cookies are shown.
  • A day-by-day plan counts back from giving day, using the keep and freeze times you enter.
  • One combined shopping list, grouped by aisle, in typical US pack sizes.
  • Prints what's-inside box cards, the schedule and the shopping list.
  • One offline HTML file plus a 3-page PDF quick-start guide.

Who it's for

Two-parent households planning Christmas for two or more kids, who also bake cookie boxes for neighbours, teachers or a cookie exchange.

Good to know

  • Gift tracker: tested in LibreOffice. Built only with functions Excel and Google Sheets support, but not tested directly in either. Separate copies don't sync. It helps you see the balance; it can't make things perfectly even. Recordkeeping only, not financial advice.
  • Cookie planner: tested in Chrome. It saves in your browser on that device, with no account. Pack sizes are typical US sizes, not exact store prices. You enter your own keep and freeze times; it gives no food-safety advice. It's a browser file, not a spreadsheet.
